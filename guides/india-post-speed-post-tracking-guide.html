India Post Speed Post Tracking: Reading the 13-Character Number

Updated 9 November 2026 · Hrudai Store Kart

India Post numbers are the most structured of any Indian courier, because they follow an international standard rather than a company format. Once you can read the pattern, you can tell what kind of article you have before you even look up its status.

The 13-character structure

Every India Post consignment number is exactly thirteen characters: two letters, nine digits, then IN. For example EE123456789IN.

  • Positions 1-2 - the service prefix. Two letters that say what product this is.
  • Positions 3-11 - the article's serial number. The last of these is a check digit.
  • Positions 12-13 - always IN, the country code for India.

Spaces and hyphens are ignored, so a number written as "EE 123 456 789 IN" is the same article. Anything that is not thirteen characters is either mistyped or not an India Post number at all.

What the prefix tells you

PrefixService
EE / EUSpeed Post (domestic)
RX / RARegistered Post
CPExpress Parcel
EM / EAEMS (international)
PPPassport consignment

A number beginning EE is domestic Speed Post. If yours begins EM, it is an international EMS item, which moves through customs and will take longer.

Three ways to track it

  1. Website - indiapost.gov.in, Track & Trace, enter the number, complete the captcha. This shows the full movement history and is the best option when something looks stuck.
  2. SMS - send POST TRACK EE123456789IN to 166 or 51969. Works without internet.
  3. App - the Dak Sewa 2.0 app, which replaced the older Postinfo app.

There is also a toll-free line, 1800-266-6868, open 9 AM to 6 PM Monday to Saturday.

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaning
Item bookedAccepted at the counter, number issued
Item receivedReached the first post office for processing
Item baggedPacked into a dispatch bag for the next leg
Item dispatchedLeft for the next office or hub
Out for DeliveryWith the postman for delivery today
Item DeliveredHanded over and recorded

Why the tracking can be blank

India Post keeps online tracking for roughly the last 60 days, so very old articles drop out of the system. There is also a lag between a scan at the counter and it appearing online, and in rural areas that lag is longer because scans are batched. A blank result does not mean the article was not posted.

What you cannot do

India Post has no lookup by name, address or phone number. The consignment number is the only key, and one wrong character returns "no record found". Keep the booking receipt until the article is delivered - it is the only copy of the number you will have.

Speed Post against the private couriers

Speed Post reaches every pin code in the country, including places no private courier will go, because it uses the post office network. That reach is the reason to choose it. The trade-off is that scans happen at post offices rather than at every handover, so the timeline has fewer entries and moves in bigger steps.

For a business shipping to a district town, Speed Post is often the only option that actually delivers. For a metro to metro parcel, a private courier will usually be faster and will show more detail along the way.

Booking at the counter: what to get right

  • Give the recipient's mobile number at booking - India Post sends a free delivery SMS to it.
  • Keep the booking receipt. The consignment number is printed on it and nowhere else.
  • Declare the value if the contents matter. Compensation on an undeclared article is limited.
  • Ask for the delivery SLA for that route; it varies by distance and by whether the destination is a metro.

What to do when an article stalls

Look at the last scan's office. If it says "bagged" and nothing follows for several days, the bag is waiting for transport, which is a capacity issue rather than a lost article. If it says "received" at the destination office and stops, the parcel is sitting there and a visit or a call to that office resolves it faster than the helpline.

Why the same number can show nothing on a third-party site

India Post publishes tracking through its own portal behind a captcha, and it does not offer an open API to third parties. Sites that claim to show live India Post status are either scraping the official page or caching old results, and both fail silently. If a third-party tracker shows nothing for an India Post article, the article is usually fine - the tool simply cannot see it.
