APSRTC Cargo Tracking: Why It Asks for a Mobile Number

Updated 9 November 2026 · Hrudai Store Kart

APSRTC Logistics moves parcels on the state bus network rather than through dedicated freight vehicles. It is cheap and reaches places couriers do not, but its tracking works differently from every private courier, and that surprises people.

Two things make APSRTC different

First, the mobile number is mandatory. APSRTC's tracking page asks for the AWB and either the sender's or the receiver's mobile number. You cannot look up a consignment with the AWB alone. The number is the second key that proves the enquiry is yours.

Second, there is no public API. APSRTC does not provide tracking data to AfterShip, TrackingMore or any other aggregator. Any website claiming to track APSRTC is either forwarding you to APSRTC's own form or asking for your details to look it up by hand. None of them have direct access.

How to check a consignment

  1. Open the APSRTC Logistics tracking page.
  2. Enter the AWB number from the booking receipt.
  3. Enter the sender's or the receiver's mobile number - either works.
  4. Submit and read the status.

Expect sparse updates

Because parcels travel on scheduled bus routes, scans happen at booking offices and destination points rather than continuously. A consignment may show nothing between dispatch and arrival. This is normal for APSRTC and does not indicate a problem.

What to keep from the booking

  • The AWB or consignment number.
  • The mobile number given at booking - either party's will do.
  • The booking office and the destination office.
  • The date of booking, useful when you need to escalate.

If the parcel is late or missing

APSRTC runs a cargo complaints line for delayed, lost or damaged consignments. Have the consignment number and the booking office ready - the destination office can trace it internally even when the public page shows nothing.

Can a tracker help at all?

For APSRTC, a tracker can store the AWB and the mobile number together so you do not retype them each time, and can hand you to the official page in one tap. It cannot show a live timeline, because the data is not published. Any tool that claims otherwise is guessing.

What APSRTC Cargo is good at

It uses the state bus network, so it reaches towns and mandals that private couriers serve only through a partner. For heavy or bulky consignments over short distances within Andhra Pradesh, it is usually the cheapest option by a wide margin.

What it is not good at

  • Speed. Parcels wait for the next scheduled bus, not for a dedicated vehicle.
  • Visibility. Scans are manual, at booking and destination offices.
  • Door delivery. Available only in selected locations and for consignments under a weight limit.

Booking, briefly

Take the consignment to a cargo booking office, declare the contents and value, give a sender and receiver mobile number, and collect the receipt. The mobile numbers you give at booking are the ones that will work on the tracking page later - if you gave a wrong one, tracking becomes impossible until you visit the office.

Keeping records that matter

Photograph the receipt and the parcel before it is accepted. APSRTC claims for damage are decided largely on what was declared and recorded at booking, and a photo taken at the counter is worth more than any argument later.
