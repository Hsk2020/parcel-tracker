Delhivery Tracking: Find Your AWB and Read the Status

Updated 9 November 2026 · Hrudai Store Kart

Delhivery is one of India's largest third-party logistics networks and carries parcels for most large marketplaces as well as small sellers. Its tracking is among the more reliable in the country, which makes an empty result more informative than usual: if Delhivery shows nothing, the parcel usually has not been picked up yet.

Where to find your Delhivery AWB

  • In the dispatch email or SMS from the seller, usually a day after the order.
  • On the physical label, printed next to a barcode, often under "AWB" or "Waybill".
  • In the seller's order page, under shipment details - not the order ID itself.

A Delhivery AWB is normally 12 to 14 digits with no letters. Numbers beginning with 1904 or similar long prefixes are common for e-commerce surface shipments.

What the scans mean

Manifested or "Shipment data received"

The seller has told Delhivery about the parcel, but Delhivery has not physically taken it. Nothing is moving yet. This can sit unchanged for a day.

Picked up / In transit

The parcel is in Delhivery's network. Expect several of these as it passes through hubs. The location shown is the hub that scanned it, not the truck's position.

Out for delivery

It is with a delivery agent for that day. If it does not arrive, the next scan is usually a failed-attempt note.

Delivered

Recorded against a signature or OTP. If you did not receive it, ask for the proof of delivery - Delhivery keeps the name and time of whoever accepted it.

Why a Delhivery parcel goes quiet

  • Between hubs. Long surface legs produce no scans until the next hub.
  • Weekends and festivals. One to two day gaps are routine.
  • Address issues. A hold for an incomplete address often appears as a non-delivery status rather than an error.
  • RTO. Return to origin after repeated failed attempts. The scans then run backwards toward the seller.

Checking a Delhivery number without an account

Delhivery's own tracking page accepts an AWB directly and needs no login. You can also paste the number into a tracker that reads Delhivery's data, which is useful when you are following several parcels from different couriers at once.

Cash on delivery and address changes

A COD parcel cannot have its address changed once it is out for delivery; the agent carries a fixed route. Before that point, the seller - not you - must raise the change with Delhivery, because the booking belongs to their account. This is the single most common reason people are told "we cannot change it".

When a Delhivery parcel is genuinely stuck

Escalate in this order, and give the AWB every time:

  1. The seller, who has a business account and a named account manager.
  2. Delhivery's own customer care, quoting the last scan location.
  3. A written complaint if the parcel is insured or high value - a phone call leaves no record.

Five working days with no scan is the usual threshold at which a courier will open a trace. Before that, they will ask you to wait.

Reading the estimated delivery date honestly

Delhivery's ETA is computed from the service level and the distance, not from live conditions. It is usually right to within a day for metro routes and routinely wrong for remote pin codes. Treat it as a range, not a promise.
