DTDC Tracking: Number Formats and What Each Scan Means

Updated 9 November 2026 · Hrudai Store Kart

DTDC is one of the oldest courier networks in India and still handles a large share of small-business and B2B consignments. Its tracking numbers are less uniform than Delhivery's, which is the main reason people struggle to identify them.

What a DTDC number looks like

PatternExample shapeTypical use
D + 7 to 11 digitsD3504296090Surface and premium express
V, X or Z + digitsV12345678Older consignment series
DTDC + alphanumericsDTDC12345678Account and bulk bookings

DTDC also prints a separate consignment number on some receipts. That number is for the booking office, not for public tracking - use the AWB.

Reading a DTDC scan

DTDC's scan text often arrives as two parts joined by a comma - the status, then a longer description with a timestamp. For example:

Out for Delivery,Shipment is out for Delivery 2026-08-31 11:39:10

Both halves say the same thing. What matters is the first part: Out for Delivery. A well-built tracker shows only that, because the timestamp is already displayed beside it.

Why a DTDC timeline looks duplicated

DTDC data reaches third-party trackers through aggregators, and the same physical scan is often reported more than once - once as an origin event, once as a destination event, and again in the event history. An aggregator that does not de-duplicate will show you the same scan two or three times, which makes a delivered parcel look like it was delivered twice. This is a display problem, not a real second delivery.

Common DTDC statuses

  • Booked - the consignment is registered, not yet moved.
  • On the way - in the network between facilities.
  • Reached destination hub - at the delivering branch.
  • At processing centre - being sorted for the delivery run.
  • Out for delivery - with the delivery agent.
  • Delivered - completed. Ask for the POD if you did not receive it.
  • Undelivered / RTO - a failed attempt, or return to origin.

If DTDC shows nothing

Give it a day. DTDC bookings made at a branch counter often appear only after the consignment reaches the first sorting facility. If it has been more than two working days, call the booking branch with the consignment number rather than the AWB.

Surface versus premium: why the timeline differs

DTDC runs both a surface network and an air express service. A surface consignment can take twice as long and produces fewer scans, because it moves on long road legs between regional hubs. If you were quoted three days and the parcel is on surface, five is normal.

If the tracking page rejects your number

  • Remove any spaces or dashes - the field expects the raw string.
  • Check for O versus 0 and I versus 1. DTDC numbers use digits; a letter that looks like a digit is usually the mistake.
  • Use the AWB, not the consignment number printed on the booking receipt.
  • Branch bookings sometimes take a day to appear in the public system.

Proof of delivery

DTDC records who accepted a delivered consignment and when. If the status says delivered and you have nothing, ask the booking branch for the POD - it names the recipient and the time, which settles most disputes without an argument.

Insurance and declared value

DTDC's liability on an undeclared consignment is capped and low. For anything valuable, declare the value at booking and keep the receipt - a claim without a declared value usually recovers only the freight charge.
