Hans Courier Tracking: Which Hans Do You Have?

Updated 9 November 2026 · Hrudai Store Kart

Searching for "Hans courier tracking" is confusing for a good reason: at least three separate Indian businesses use the name, and they are in completely different lines of work. Sending you to the wrong one wastes your time, so start by working out which you have.

The three companies

If your parcel is...You want
A courier or parcel sent from Mumbai or abroadHans Air Express
Sent by bus, with an LR or docket number from a travel agencyHans Travels
An online order from hanskart.inHans Kart store tracking

Hans Air Express - the courier

An international and domestic courier based in Mumbai, operating from Chandivali in Andheri East. It handles documents, parcels, bulk shipments and air cargo, with same-day delivery inside Mumbai and delivery to over 200 countries internationally.

Tracking is on hansairexpress.com. You will need the consignment or AWB number from the booking receipt or the dispatch email. Customer care is on +91 9029642249 and +91 9619544843, and by email at hansairexpress@gmail.com.

Hans Travels - the bus parcel

This is a transport and travel operator, not a courier. Parcels move on its bus network and are tracked by LR number (lorry receipt) or docket number, exactly like VRL or APSRTC Cargo. The LR is issued at the booking counter and printed on the receipt.

If your number looks like a short LR rather than a courier AWB, this is the one you have. The booking counter is the fastest route to a status - bus parcel networks rarely publish live tracking to third parties.

Hans Kart - the online store

If you placed an order on hanskart.in, your reference is an order ID, not a courier tracking number. No third-party tracker can resolve it. Use the store's own order tracking page, which usually needs the order ID plus your registered phone or email. Once the parcel is dispatched, the shipping message carries the actual courier AWB - that is what you enter into a tracker.

How to tell them apart in ten seconds

  • Long alphanumeric code, came by email or from a courier counter - Hans Air Express
  • Short numeric LR from a travel agency, parcel travelled by bus - Hans Travels
  • Order ID like OD or a marketplace reference - Hans Kart, use the store's page

If you still are not sure

Look at who took your money. A courier invoice, a bus parcel receipt and an online order confirmation look very different, and the document tells you which company you are dealing with. When in doubt, call the number on that document rather than searching the name - it reaches the right company in one step.

Why the confusion exists

India has no central registry of courier names, so unrelated businesses end up sharing one. "Hans" is a common business name in western and central India, and three quite different operations have built on it: a Mumbai courier, an Indore-based travel and transport operator, and an online store in Secunderabad. Search engines treat them as one topic, which is why the results for "hans courier tracking" mix all three.

The practical consequence is simple: if a tracking page returns nothing, the chances are you are on the wrong company's site, not that your parcel is missing.

Tracking an international shipment

If Hans Air Express is carrying a parcel into India from abroad, expect two separate records. The overseas sender's system tracks it until it leaves the origin country, and the Indian record begins once it clears customs. A gap between the two is normal and does not mean the parcel has been lost - it is sitting in a customs queue.

Duty, if any, is assessed on the declared value at the time of arrival. If the declared value is wrong, the parcel can be held pending a correction, and that delay never shows on a tracking page.

When a bus-parcel LR is the right number

Bus parcel networks - Hans Travels among them - issue an LR at the counter and hand you a carbon copy. That copy is the only record you will have. There is usually no online tracking, and the receiving branch will ask for the LR number when you collect. Photograph the receipt before the parcel leaves your hands.

What none of the three can do

None of these companies can change an address after dispatch, redirect a parcel mid-route, or cancel a booking once it is scanned. If you need any of those, it has to be arranged by the sender, at the booking branch, before the parcel moves. After that, the only options are delivery or return.
