VRL Logistics Tracking: Finding and Using Your LR Number

Updated 9 November 2026 · Hrudai Store Kart

VRL Logistics moves goods by road across India through a branch network rather than a hub-and-spoke courier model. That changes how you track it: the key is an LR number from the Lorry Receipt, not a courier AWB.

LR number versus Courier No.

VRL's tracking page has two tabs. The LR No. tab is for goods consignments booked at a branch - this is what almost everyone needs. The Courier No. tab is for VRL's parcel courier product, which issues a different series. Entering a Courier No. into the LR tab returns nothing, which is the most common reason people conclude their consignment "does not exist".

An LR number is normally numeric, around ten digits, and the field accepts up to twenty. It is printed on the Lorry Receipt you received at booking.

Where to find your LR number

  • On the Lorry Receipt / Goods Consignment Note issued at the booking branch.
  • In the booking confirmation from the sender, if they booked on your behalf.
  • On the carton label, usually beside a barcode.

If you only have the sender's name or mobile number, the public page cannot help - VRL does not offer lookup by name or phone. Ask the sender for the LR number, or call the booking branch.

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaning
BookedRegistered at a branch, LR issued, not yet moved
In transitMoving between branches by road
Arrived at destination branchAt the branch nearest the receiver
Out for deliveryOn a vehicle for door delivery
Ready for collectionWaiting at the branch for the consignee
DeliveredHanded over and recorded

Why updates are sparse

VRL scans at branch level, not continuously. A consignment on a long road leg can show nothing for a day or more, and a single "in transit" entry may cover several hundred kilometres. This is normal for surface transport and is not a sign of a problem.

Collecting the consignment

For godown delivery, take the original LR copy and a valid photo ID. If someone else collects on your behalf, they need an authorisation letter from you as well. Commercial consignments may additionally require GST-related documents - check before travelling to the branch, because the counter will not release goods without them.

If nothing shows at all

  • Confirm you are using the LR number, not the Courier No. or an invoice number.
  • A booking made in the last few hours may not have reached the public system yet.
  • Check the digits against the receipt - a single wrong digit returns "no data found".
  • If it still fails, call the booking branch with the receipt in hand.

VRL's place in Indian logistics

VRL runs one of the largest owned goods-transport fleets in the country and a branch network covering most districts. It is built for consignments of goods rather than for e-commerce parcels: you book at a branch, the consignment travels on scheduled road services, and it is collected from a destination branch or delivered locally.

Door delivery versus godown delivery

Booking includes a choice that decides how the consignment ends. Door delivery means a local vehicle brings it to the address, and the timeline will show an out-for-delivery entry. Godown delivery means it stops at the destination branch and waits for you - the status will say ready for collection, and it will stay there until someone collects it. A consignment showing "ready for collection" for a week is not delayed; nobody has gone to get it.

Who to call, and when

VRL's customer care is reachable on a toll-free line and by email, but the fastest route for a specific consignment is usually the booking branch or the destination branch, because they can see the physical register. Quote the LR number and the booking date. Escalate to customer care only when a branch cannot resolve it.

Why third-party trackers struggle with VRL

VRL does not publish tracking data to aggregators, so a third-party site either forwards you to VRL's own page or scrapes it - and scraping breaks whenever VRL changes the page. There is no way for an outside tool to show a live VRL timeline reliably. The official page is the only dependable source.
