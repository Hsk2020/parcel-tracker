How to Track Any Parcel in India

Updated 9 November 2026 · Hrudai Store Kart

Almost every parcel in India can be tracked, but the tracking number is not always where you expect it, and not every number you are given is a tracking number at all. This guide covers where to find the right number and what each type of number can do.

Find the right number first

Three different numbers arrive with an online order, and only one of them tracks the parcel:

  • The order ID - what the shop calls your purchase. Amazon uses the 404-1234567-1234567 format. This is not a tracking number; no courier recognises it.
  • The courier AWB - the waybill the courier prints on the label. This is the number that works. It appears in the dispatch email or SMS, usually a day after the order confirmation.
  • The store's own reference - codes starting with OD or similar. Internal to the seller.

If your confirmation email shows only an order ID, the parcel has not been handed to a courier yet. The AWB follows when it ships.

These are the patterns couriers use in practice. They are conventions, not a published standard, so treat them as a strong hint rather than a rule.

When a number is ambiguous

A bare 10 or 11 digit number could belong to more than one courier. If the status looks wrong or empty, select the courier manually instead of relying on automatic detection. This is the single most common cause of "my parcel is not showing".

Why a parcel shows nothing at all

  • The label was just created. The number exists before the courier scans it. Twelve to twenty-four hours of silence is normal.
  • The courier scans only at hubs. A quiet day in transit is not a lost parcel.
  • The seller used a local transport service. Bus cargo and city couriers often have no online tracking at all.
  • The number has a typo. Compare it character by character against the label.

What you can and cannot track

Couriers that publish tracking to third parties - Delhivery, Ekart, XpressBees, Blue Dart, DTDC, Shadowfax, India Post, Cainiao, UPS and Amazon - can be followed in one place. Others, including Porter, Rapido and APSRTC Cargo, keep tracking inside their own app or behind a form. For those, no third-party tool can show you a live timeline; the honest answer is to open the courier's own page.

Tracking when you have no tracking number

If the seller never sent one, the number still exists somewhere. Check, in this order: the dispatch email (search your inbox for the courier's name), the SMS from the seller, the order page under "shipment details", and finally the seller's support - they can read the AWB off their own dashboard in seconds.

What does not work: giving a courier your name and address. No courier in India will search by recipient name over the phone, because it would expose other customers' parcels.

Following several parcels at once

If you order often, keep the numbers in one place rather than reopening five different courier sites. A tracker that stores your list in the browser lets you see every parcel's last scan side by side, and flags the ones that moved since you last looked.

Why the same parcel shows different statuses on two sites

Courier data reaches third-party trackers through an aggregator, and aggregators refresh on their own schedule - often every few hours. The courier's own site is always fresher. If the two disagree, trust the courier and treat the third-party view as a summary.
