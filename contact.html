Before you write - three quick answers

"My tracking has not updated for two days"

Normal on road routes. Couriers scan at hubs, not continuously. Five working days without a scan is when it is worth chasing - and the sender, who holds the booking, gets a faster answer than you will.

"It says delivered but I have nothing"

Check the reception desk, security and neighbours first. Then ask the sender to request proof of delivery from the courier - it records who signed and when, and settles most disputes in a day.

"This site showed the wrong status"

Status is passed through from the courier and can lag by minutes to hours. The courier's own page is always fresher. If you can send the tracking number and what you saw, we will look at whether it is a bug on our side.
