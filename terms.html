HSK Parcel Tracker · Hrudai Store Kart

The short version

HSK Parcel Tracker is a free tool that shows the delivery status of a parcel by looking up its tracking number with the courier. It is an information service, not a courier. We do not carry your parcel, we cannot change its route, and we cannot make it arrive faster.

The status you see comes from the courier's own systems. It can lag, and it can be wrong. Always treat the courier's own website as the authoritative record, and the physical parcel as the final proof.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What this service is

We take a tracking number, work out which courier it belongs to, and display the status and scan history that the courier reports. Where the courier publishes an estimated delivery date, we show it. Where a courier does not publish tracking data to third parties, we link you to that courier's own page instead of guessing.

What we do not promise

  • Accuracy. Status and timestamps are passed through from the courier. We do not verify them independently.
  • Freshness. Data can lag the courier by minutes to hours, and some couriers update only at hubs.
  • Coverage. Not every courier, and not every consignment, is available. Some return no data at all.
  • Availability. The service is provided as-is. It may be unavailable during maintenance or when an upstream provider fails.
  • Delivery outcomes. We have no control over whether, when, or where a parcel is delivered.

Your responsibilities

  • Only look up tracking numbers you are entitled to look up. A tracking number can reveal a recipient's city and delivery progress.

  • Do not attempt to overload, scrape, or interfere with the service or its upstream providers.
  • Do not resell or redistribute the tracking data you obtain here. Data belongs to the courier that produced it.
