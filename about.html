About HSK Parcel Tracker

Hrudai Store Kart

What this tool does

HSK Parcel Tracker is a free shipment tracking page run by Hrudai Store Kart. You paste a courier tracking number and it shows the parcel's current status and its scan history, in one place, without needing an account.

It works with the couriers we and our customers use most: Delhivery, XpressBees, Shadowfax, Blue Dart, DTDC, Ekart, India Post and UPS, plus roughly 1,700 more through our tracking partners.

How it works

  • The courier is identified from the format of the tracking number.
  • Live status is fetched from a tracking provider (AfterShip, with TrackingMore as a fallback).
  • Parcels you add are saved in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • Where a courier publishes no data to tracking providers, we link you straight to that courier's own tracking page.

What it costs

Nothing. The page is free to use and is supported by advertising. We do not charge for tracking and we do not sell shipping data.

Who we are

Hrudai Store Kart is an Indian online retailer operating two stores: hsk2020.in for fashion and accessories, and hsk.co.in for organic products. This tracker exists so customers can follow their orders without hunting through courier websites.
