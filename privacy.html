Privacy Policy

HSK Parcel Tracker · Hrudai Store Kart

Short version

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, address or phone number. The tracking numbers you look up are saved only in your own browser. The site shows advertising, and those ads use cookies set by Google.

What we collect

  • Tracking numbers you enter. These are sent to our tracking provider so the status can be fetched. They are saved in your browser's local storage so your list survives a page refresh.
  • Nothing else. No login, no email collection, no contact form, no user profiles.

What stays on your device

Your saved parcel list, and any preference you set, live in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them. We cannot see this list, and it is not uploaded anywhere.

Third parties we send a tracking number to

To show live status, the tracking number you enter is sent to our tracking provider: AfterShip (aftership.com), with TrackingMore (trackingmore.com) as a fallback. These providers look the number up with the courier and return the status. We do not send them any information about you beyond the number itself.

If you tap a courier's own tracking link, you leave our site and that courier's privacy policy applies.

Cookies and advertising

This site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and others on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Google's practices are described at Google's advertising policies.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can clear saved parcels from within the app at any time (open a parcel and tap Remove), or clear your browser's site data. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the tracker itself will still work.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date.
